How a receipt is made

  1. 1

    Publish

    SMT's committee writes its call for each coin to SMT World's public feed: LONG, SHORT or WAIT, a conviction, and a three-sentence reason.

  2. 2

    Seal

    A keeper seals the whole snapshot on Monad: every coin, WAITs included, plus the hash of each reason. The contract refuses an old, repeated or partial snapshot.

  3. 3

    Price

    Entry is the first RedStone tick after the seal block. Exit is entry plus four hours. RedStone signs a price every 10 seconds; the contract checks three of its five signers itself.

  4. 4

    Grade

    Anyone can submit the two signed prices. The contract computes the move, subtracts the fee and keeps score. A call nobody grades within 22 hours expires as a miss.

What can't be faked

  • Hindsight. The entry price is signed after the call is on-chain.
  • Cherry-picking. Every coin is sealed every time, and a call left ungraded counts against the bot.
  • The price. The contract checks RedStone's signatures at the exact second. It never takes a number from us.
  • The reason. Each call's reason is hashed into the seal. Tap "Verify" on any line to recompute it in your browser.
  • Edits. There is no owner, no admin key and no upgrade path.
